LEVEL 1: BEGINNER

Lesson 1: Introduction to Cybersecurity

PART A – ENGLISH

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, mobile devices, networks, applications and digital information from unauthorized access, attacks, damage or theft.

Example: When you protect your phone with a strong password and avoid suspicious links, you are practising basic cybersecurity.

Why is Cybersecurity Important?

  • It protects personal information.
  • It helps prevent financial fraud.
  • It protects organizations and businesses.
  • It helps keep computer systems available and reliable.

PART B – HAUSA

Menene Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity na nufin hanyoyin kare kwamfuta, wayar hannu, network, manhajoji da bayanan sirri daga shiga ba tare da izini ba, hare-haren yanar gizo, lalacewa ko satar bayanai.

Misali: Idan ka saka password mai ƙarfi a wayarka kuma ka guji danna links da ba ka yarda da su ba, kana yin aikin kariya ta yanar gizo.

Me ya sa Cybersecurity yake da muhimmanci?

  • Yana kare bayanan mutum.
  • Yana taimakawa wajen hana zamba ta kuɗi.
  • Yana kare kamfanoni da cibiyoyi.
  • Yana taimakawa tsarin kwamfuta ya ci gaba da aiki lafiya.

Important Terms – Muhimman Kalmomi

  • Cyber: Abubuwan da suka shafi duniyar kwamfuta da intanet.
  • Security: Tsaro ko kariya.
  • Threat: Barazana.
  • Attack: Hari.
  • Data: Bayanai.
  • Unauthorized access: Shiga ba tare da izini ba.
